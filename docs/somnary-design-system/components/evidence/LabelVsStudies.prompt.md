The choreographed motif: the bottle's claim renders, a line draws through it, the studies' finding fades in beneath with its citation chip.

```jsx
<LabelVsStudies claim="Fall asleep 3× faster"
  found="The studies found about 7 minutes, on average."
  chip={{ finding: 'People fell asleep about 7 minutes sooner than with placebo.', people: 1683, year: 2013 }} />
```

`animate={false}` for static comps. Reduced motion renders the final state immediately.
