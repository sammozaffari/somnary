The citation chip every claim carries. Click opens a popover: the finding in one plain sentence, people count, year, outbound link, and when the link was last checked.

```jsx
Falls asleep about 7 minutes faster{' '}
<StudyChip finding="People taking melatonin fell asleep about 7 minutes sooner than people taking a placebo."
  people={1683} year={2013} url="https://doi.org/..." linkText="Read the review (19 studies)"
  lastChecked="1 August 2026" />
```

`defaultOpen` for static mocks. Popover is 300px, opens below-left.
