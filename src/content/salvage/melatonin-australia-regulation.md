# Melatonin regulatory status in Australia (DRAFT — not promoted)

**Claim (short).** Melatonin is prescription-only in Australia (TGA regulatory statement).

**Statement (verbatim from origin).** "Melatonin is prescription-only in Australia — the
melatonin products here are personal imports, not sold over the counter."

**Citation added at extraction.** TGA Poisons Standard (SUSMP): melatonin is Schedule 4
(Prescription Only Medicine).

> **Reviewer flag — do not promote as-is.** The flat "prescription-only" wording predates
> (or overlooks) the 2021 down-scheduling: since 1 June 2021 a narrow Schedule 3
> (Pharmacist Only) exemption exists for **modified-release melatonin ≤2 mg, adults 55+**,
> short supply. So "prescription-only" is true for the general case but not absolute. The
> consuming session (safety page / product layer) must (a) resolve the exact current
> Poisons Standard instrument on the Federal Register of Legislation, and (b) decide
> whether to carry the S3 exemption nuance rather than the flat statement. Carried here
> as a draft, not a fact.
