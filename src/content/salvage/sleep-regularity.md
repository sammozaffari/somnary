# Sleep regularity (DRAFT — not promoted)

**Claim (short).** Sleep regularity predicts mortality more strongly than duration
(observational).

**Finding (verbatim from origin).** Prospective UK Biobank cohort of ~60,000 participants
with one week of accelerometry: a Sleep Regularity Index (day-to-day consistency of the
sleep–wake pattern) was more strongly predictive of all-cause, cardiometabolic, and
cancer mortality than sleep duration. This is an observational association, not evidence
that changing one's schedule causes the outcome. — PMID 37738616 / DOI 10.1093/sleep/zsad253

**What it does not show.** A cohort study: association, not causation. It cannot prove
that making a schedule more regular *causes* better sleep or a longer life; people who
keep regular hours may differ in many other ways.
