# Caffeine timing (DRAFT — not promoted)

**Claim (short).** Caffeine taken even 6 hours before bed disrupts sleep (controlled trial).

**Finding (verbatim from origin).** Randomised, double-blind study: a 400 mg dose of
caffeine (roughly a few cups of coffee) taken at bedtime, 3 hours before, or 6 hours
before bed all produced significant disruption of sleep versus placebo; the reduction
in total sleep time even at the 6-hour interval was substantial enough that the authors
supported avoiding significant caffeine consumption for at least 6 hours before bedtime.
— PMID 24235903 / DOI 10.5664/jcsm.3170

**What it does not show.** One fixed dose in a modest sample; it doesn't set a personal
cut-off time, and doesn't capture how tolerance, genetics, and usual intake shift the
picture. "Six hours" is a finding about that dose in those participants, not a rule.
